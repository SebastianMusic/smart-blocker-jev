Your intention

Strict mode

Every page is covered by a “Checking this page…” screen (and muted) while it is being checked. Normally a page that can't be checked — rate limit, API error, timeout — is shown anyway. With this on, it stays covered with a Show page anyway button.

Model endpoint

Where page checks are sent. Defaults to the TypeSafe jev endpoint. Any endpoint that answers the same request shape works — self-hosted and open-source models included.

Stored only in this browser. Sent only to the endpoint above, and only when set.

Model prompt

What jev is asked about every page. Pick a starting point, then edit the words. Changing any of this clears cached verdicts.

Choosing a preset replaces the three boxes below with its wording. Custom leaves your own wording alone.

Search pages, listings and comparison pages lead both ways: they can serve your intention, and waste your time. The default wording treats them as a match and leaves the blocking to your own domain rules and to the strictness line below. Pick Strict if you want those pages blocked unless they are clearly about your intention.

Strictness

jev scores how likely each page is to match your intention. Pages scoring below this line are blocked. Changing it clears cached verdicts.

Domain rules

One rule per line. Rules apply without calling jev.

A domain like youtube.com also covers its subdomains. A regular expression between slashes, like /youtube\.com\/shorts/ or /^(www\.)?reddit\.com$/, is matched case-insensitively against both the hostname and the full URL.

Domains containing "bank" are always skipped.
